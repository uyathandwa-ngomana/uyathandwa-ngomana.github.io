# User Stories and Tasks — Reflection

[← Back to portfolio](../README.md) · [View Evidence](index.md)

## What I produced

I documented 18 user stories across different areas of CommunityPulse AI and ProKonecta. The stories covered application functionality and testing, customer registration and login, service requests, professional discovery, profiles, verification, quotes, chat, jobs, and backend reliability. I included acceptance criteria and tasks to make the expected outcomes and work involved clearer.

## What I learned

Writing user stories from the user's point of view helped me understand the purpose of each feature and who would benefit from it. For ProKonecta, I considered both customers looking for service providers and professionals looking for new work. Acceptance criteria helped me identify what should happen for a feature to work as expected, while tasks broke the work into smaller steps that could be developed and tested.

## Challenges

One challenge was covering a system with several connected features without making the stories too broad. I had to separate the customer journey, professional features, and backend reliability work into smaller stories. Another consideration was distinguishing the expected behaviour of a feature from what I had personally tested. Some features still required further testing, so writing acceptance criteria did not automatically mean that the feature was complete.

## What I would do differently

I would write the user stories and agree on their acceptance criteria earlier in the development process. I would also link each story to its development tasks and testing evidence, updating its status as work progresses. This would make it easier to track progress, identify unfinished work, and show which requirements had actually been tested.
