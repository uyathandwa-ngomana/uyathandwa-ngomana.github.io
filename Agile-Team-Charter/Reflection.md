# Agile Team Charter — Reflection

[← Back to portfolio](../README.md) · [View Evidence](index.md)

## What I produced

I created an Agile Team Charter for my projects, CommunityPulse AI and ProKonecta. It outlines the project purpose, team roles, working agreements, communication, decision-making, Definition of Done, and the main risks and constraints. It also identifies my role as a student developer, Lesego Lebese as a team member, and Palesa as the supervisor.

## What I learned

I learned that an Agile Team Charter helps clarify responsibilities and establish expectations for how project work is completed. It also highlights the importance of communication, testing, teamwork, and resolving technical issues. Defining what it means for a task to be complete helped me understand why features should be tested before being considered finished.

## Challenges

One of the challenges was resolving backend errors in ProKonecta and checking that different parts of the system worked correctly. These issues required investigation and testing. I also learned that keeping responsibilities clear and recording progress helps the team understand what has been completed and what still needs attention.

## What I would do differently

I would review the charter with the team and supervisor at the beginning of the project to make sure everyone understands their responsibilities. I would also update it when project requirements or priorities change and document decisions more consistently throughout development.
