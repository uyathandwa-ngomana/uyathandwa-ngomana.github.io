# Sprint Backlog — Reflection

[← Back to portfolio](../README.md) · [View Evidence](index.md)

## What I produced

I organised my work on CommunityPulse AI and ProKonecta into sprint goals and tasks. The backlog covers development, backend implementation, testing, debugging and fixing errors.

## What I learned

Creating the sprint backlog helped me understand the importance of breaking project work into smaller tasks and tracking progress. I learned that development involves more than adding features. Testing the application and fixing backend errors are also important for making sure the system works as expected.

## Challenges

One challenge was identifying and resolving backend errors while testing different parts of ProKonecta. Some features worked, while others still had issues that needed further investigation. Balancing development and testing also showed me the importance of setting clear goals and tracking unfinished tasks.

## What I would do differently

I would define sprint goals at the beginning of each work period and review my progress regularly. I would also leave time for unexpected errors and testing so that changes to the plan can be recorded and managed more effectively.
