# Product Backlog — Reflection

[← Back to portfolio](../README.md) · [View Evidence](index.md)

## What I produced

I created a Product Backlog for CommunityPulse AI and ProKonecta. It lists the main development tasks, their priorities, estimated story points and current statuses.

## What I learned

Creating the backlog helped me understand how to organise tasks according to their importance and track progress during development. I learned that essential features, such as backend functionality, authentication and service requests, need to be prioritised. I also learned that testing and fixing errors are important parts of development because they help identify problems and improve how the system works.

## Challenges

One challenge was estimating story points because some tasks were more complex than others. It was also important to test the functionality carefully, identify backend errors and determine which features were working correctly and which still needed attention.

## What I would do differently

I would review and update the backlog more regularly as tasks are completed or new issues arise. I would also improve my story-point estimates based on the time and effort required for each task. This would make it easier to track progress and plan future work.
