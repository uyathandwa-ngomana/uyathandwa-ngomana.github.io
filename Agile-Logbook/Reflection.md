# Agile Logbook — Reflection

[← Back to portfolio](../README.md) · [View Evidence](index.md)

## What I produced

I created an Agile Logbook containing a taskboard, a walkthrough of my work, and weekly stand-up entries. The entries record the work I completed, what I planned to do next, and any challenges or issues that affected my progress on CommunityPulse AI and Prokon.

## What I learned

I learned that an Agile Logbook helps me keep track of my progress and organise my work into smaller tasks. It also helps me identify what has been completed, what still needs attention, and which issues need to be resolved. Working on these projects helped me understand the importance of development, testing, and fixing errors throughout the process.

## Challenges

One of the challenges was resolving backend errors in Prokon and checking that the different parts of the system worked correctly. Some issues required investigation and testing before I could identify what needed to be fixed. Keeping a clear record of these challenges also showed me the importance of documenting my progress.

## What I would do differently

I would update my logbook more regularly and record completed tasks and challenges as they happen. I would also include screenshots or other evidence of my work to make it easier to verify my progress and show how I resolved problems.
